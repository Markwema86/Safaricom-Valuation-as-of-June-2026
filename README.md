## 🎓 Valuation Methodology Guide

### Enterprise Value (EV)
```
EV = Market Cap + Total Debt - Cash
```
Represents the **total cost to acquire the company outright**, adjusting for leverage and cash on hand.

### EV/EBITDA Multiple
```
EV/EBITDA = Enterprise Value / EBITDA
```
Shows how many times earnings (before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization) investors are willing to pay. Commonly used in M&A and PE valuation.

### Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio
```
P/E = Share Price / Earnings Per Share (EPS)
```
Shows how many shillings investors pay for each shilling of annual profit. Used by public market investors to quickly compare valuations.

### Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)
```
Intrinsic Value = Σ(FCF_t / (1+r)^t) + Terminal Value / (1+r)^n
```
The most theoretically sound method, valuing a company as the sum of all future free cash flows discounted to present value. Used by Warren Buffett and fundamental investors.

**Key Assumptions:**
- **Discount Rate (r):** Your required annual return (typically 10-15% for stocks)
- **Terminal Growth Rate:** Long-term perpetual growth (typically 3-5%)
- **FCF Growth:** Projected annual free cash flow growth
